### CHEM-000528: Vacancy catalysts for practical low-pressure ammonia synthesis
- **Subfield:** Nitrogen fixation and ammonia chemistry
- **Open:** Maintain active anion vacancies during prolonged nitrogen–hydrogen conversion and transient operation, with useful production rates and abundant catalyst constituents.
- **Status:** Mild-condition alternatives to Haber–Bosch have been sought for a century. A 2025 study demonstrated nitrogen activation on Ba–Si oxynitride-hydride vacancies.
- **Type:** engineering
- **Why:** Lower pressure and broader catalyst choices could support smaller ammonia plants.
